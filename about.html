The Haus
How It Began

Where Velvet Meets Espresso

The Velvet Haus started with a simple wish — a café in our own neighborhood that felt as good as the coffee tasted.

So we built it: sculpted wood slats rising from the counter, soft pendants glowing overhead, deep green velvet armchairs and a pastry case filled every morning. A place for the early commuter and the slow Saturday, for first dates, study sessions and old friends catching up.

Since the day we opened our doors, we've been pouring for our neighbors — one velvet cup at a time.

Our Philosophy

We believe coffee is a ritual, a café is a living room, and every guest who walks through our door deserves to feel right at home.

01

Craft

Every espresso is pulled to order and every milk steamed with care. Our signatures are built from real ingredients — brown sugar, soursop, lavender, spice.

02

Comfort

Velvet seats, warm wood and soft light. We designed every corner so you'd want to stay just a little longer.

03

Community

We know regulars by name and their orders by heart. The Haus belongs to the neighborhood that fills it.

The Space

Every Detail, Considered

Our interior was designed as a warm cocoon of espresso-brown walls, pale timber and moss velvet. The slatted wood cascades from the ceiling like steam rising from a cup, and in the back a neon sign asks the only question that matters: what is that velvet?
