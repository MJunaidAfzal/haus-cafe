Our Story

A Soft Place to Land in a Loud City

The Velvet Haus was built around one simple idea: great coffee tastes better somewhere you actually want to stay.

Warm wood, moss-green velvet and a counter stacked with fresh bakes — every corner was designed for lingering. Stop in for a cortado on the way to the train, or settle into an armchair with a London Fog and stay a while.
